# Suspected proxy attendance

**When:** someone reports cheating, or the same students keep getting flagged.

## What Argus already stops

Screenshots and forwarded photos of the code (expire in seconds), reused
scans, scanning for a friend from your own phone, modified or rooted phones,
emulators, and scans from clearly off campus. Fake GPS is detected and
flagged.

## What needs people

- **A friend in class video-calls the code** to someone elsewhere on campus.
- **A friend carries another student's phone.**

These are caught by **spot checks** (the teacher calls a few names, suggested
by Argus, and marks Here / Not here), **rechecks** (a surprise second scan
during class), and the **headcount** (Argus warns when more students are
marked present than the teacher counted).

## Investigating a student

1. Attendance → their classes → look at the flags on each scan: fake
   location, poor location, not on campus Wi-Fi, recently changed phone,
   unverified iPhone.
2. Phones → their phone history (many changes is a warning sign).
3. Ask the teacher to include them in the next spot checks.
4. If confirmed: correct the records (two people) and follow the college's
   published proxy policy. Everything you do is in the audit log.

## Tightening the checks (admin)

Admin → **Anti-proxy checks**: raise the points for a signal (e.g. "not
campus network") or lower the "flagged" threshold so more scans go to spot
checks. Don't switch signals off except when one is clearly broken.
