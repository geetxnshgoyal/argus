# Phones

Each student marks attendance from **one registered phone**. The phone holds a
key that can't be copied, so a friend's phone always marks the friend.

All of this is on Admin → Attendance → **Phones**.

## A student got a new phone

1. The student installs Argus on the new phone and signs in. The app says the
   new phone will be ready in 48 hours; **the old phone keeps working until then**.
2. Nothing to do: it activates by itself after 48 hours.
3. Need it sooner (old phone broken)? Check the student's **ID card**, find the
   request under Phones → **Activate now**, and write a note like
   "ID checked, old phone broken".

## A request says "needs approval"

This happens when:
- the phone was registered to **another student** in the last 6 months, or
- it's an **iPhone that Apple couldn't verify** (pilot mode).

Ask the student to come to the office with their **ID card and the phone**,
with the Argus app open. Check the name in the app matches the ID, then
**Activate now** with a note. If anything looks wrong (a laptop, someone
else's phone, a mismatched name): **Reject**.

## A phone was lost or stolen

Search the student → **Remove** the phone. It stops working at once. When the
student gets a new phone they register it and you activate it (above).

## "This phone is already registered to another student"

Two students tried to share one phone. Each student needs their own phone.
If the phone really changed owner (sold or handed down), remove it from the
old owner first; the new registration will then need approval.

## "You have changed phones too many times this term"

The limit is 2 changes per term. Check the reason with the student, then
remove the old phone and activate the new one yourself.
