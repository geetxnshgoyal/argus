# ADR-0030: Classroom Wi-Fi routers as presence evidence

**Context.** GPS can't tell one classroom from the next, and a friend on campus can relay the QR code. Each classroom has its own Wi-Fi routers (the pilot college has one or two per room). Every router broadcasts all college networks (CAMPUS_STUDENTS, _STAFF, _FACULTY, _GUEST…) on two bands, so their BSSIDs differ only in the last octet.

**Decision.**
- A router is identified by the **first five octets** of its BSSID (`0a:1b:2c:78:0e`). Rooms list their routers (`rooms.wifi_routers`, edited on the Rooms page).
- At each scan the app adds the Wi-Fi it sees to the **signed** payload: Android the connected router plus nearby scan results (30 strongest); iPhone the connected router only.
- The server's verdict: `room` (one of this room's routers), `other_room` (only other rooms' routers), `campus` (college Wi-Fi from an unassigned router), `not_campus` (no college Wi-Fi), `unknown` (no data). Only the verdict is stored; the router list is never kept with the attempt.
- Soft signals, never refusals: `wifi_other_room` (15) and `wifi_not_campus` (25), adjustable on Anti-proxy checks. Phones stay connected to the router they joined (an iPhone walking from C8 to C9 still reports C8's router), so a mismatch must not reject.
- **Learning:** for verified scans (inside the campus area), the strongest college router is counted per room (`wifi_observations`). Admin → Wi-Fi routers lists these so Acad Ops can add them to rooms with one click. The college network name prefix is `app_settings.campus_wifi_ssid_prefix` (e.g. `CAMPUS`; set on the Wi-Fi routers page, empty by default).
- **iPhone:** reading Wi-Fi needs Apple's Access Wi-Fi Information entitlement, which free (personal) developer accounts can't use. Until the paid Apple Developer Program is used, iPhones report nothing and get `unknown` (no penalty). The Swift code is in place; add `com.apple.developer.networking.wifi-info` to a `Runner.entitlements` then.

**Consequences.** On Android, a relayed QR scanned from another room or off campus is flagged for spot checks. Surveying rooms is optional: routers are learned from normal use.

**Amendment (2026-10-09): "which room am I in".**
- The Android app's Home shows the room whose router it hears loudest ("You're in Classroom 6"), and warns when a class is on in a different room. The phone fetches the rooms' router lists (`GET /v1/me/wifi-rooms`; router IDs are no secret, any phone in the room sees them) and compares them with its own scan every ~30 s while the app is open (Android's limit is about four scans per two minutes). It asks for a fresh scan and waits for it, and ignores results older than 45 s: while connected, Android keeps old results, and phones stay on the router they joined in another room. The room is the one whose router is loudest, connected or not; another room must be 4 dB louder to replace the one shown, so the card doesn't flicker between neighbours. That scan never leaves the phone. Android needs precise location to read Wi-Fi, so the card offers an **Allow** button if it hasn't been granted yet.
- At each scan, the attempt also stores `wifi_room`, the room whose router was loudest. Staff see it at scan time only, never live: the teacher's roster says "Phone was near Classroom 8's Wi-Fi, not this room's" and the support evidence shows the room per scan. There is no background tracking (spec §13 consent notice).
